Draw Steel is a Tactical, Heroic, Cinematic, Fantasy Tabletop Roleplaying Game.
As of now there are only three books out for Draw Steel: Heroes, Monsters, and the Delian Tomb.

# Heroes

## Intro
A glossary of terms
descriptions of the five main characteristics: Might, Agility, Reason, Intuition, Presence
dice: 2d10 is the standard roll instead of a d20, this is called a Power Roll

## Making Characters
Ancestries: Devil, Dragon Knights, Dwarf, Wode Elf, High Elf, Hakaan, Human, Memonek, Orc, Polder, Revenant, or Time Raider
Cultures
Careers
Classes: Censor, Conduit, Elementalist, Fury, Null, Shadow, Tactician, Talent, Troubador
Kits: some classes provide specific equipment loadouts for martial combat
Leveling: character levels start at 1 and go up to 10

## Gameplay
Combat
Recoveries and Respites
Negotiation
Downtime Projects
Rewards
Help for the Director, their term for "Game Master"

## Basic Setting
Orden and the Timescape
Gods and Religion

# Monsters

## Basics of Monsters
Rules for creating Monsters and encounter building
Malice: the Director's villain resource
Monster Hierarchies: Organizations and Roles
Minions: Squads of enemies that are designed for heroic gameplay for the PCs
Retainers: friendly NPCs that can help in combat
Dynamic Terrain: map objects that make the terrain dynamic with play

## Monster Stat Blocks
Most of the book is 200 pages of monster stat blocks
some groups are: goblins, dragons, giants, kobolds, war dogs, solo monsters, undead, etc
